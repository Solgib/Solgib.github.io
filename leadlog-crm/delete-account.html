Delete Your Account

LeadLog CRM · Last updated: September 26, 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your LeadLog CRM account and all associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to solgib@yahoo.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your LeadLog CRM account
  3. We will confirm your identity and process the deletion within 30 days

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account information (name, email, phone number, password)
  • All leads you have created
  • All customer records
  • All appointments
  • All sales records and sales-pitch notes
  • Your app preferences (theme, language)
This action is permanent. Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered.
